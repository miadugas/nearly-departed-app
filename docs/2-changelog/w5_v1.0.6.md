## Changes

- **Died here mode** (`src/lib/wikidata.ts`, `src/hooks/use-nearby-souls.ts`): `buildQuery`/`fetchNearbySouls` take `mode: "buried" | "died"`; died-mode swaps the nearest-first subquery predicate from P119 (place of burial) to P20 (place of death). Both modes now also fetch the complementary place (`Soul.otherPlace`) and stamp `Soul.placeKind`. React-query key includes the mode so caches never collide.
- **Legit-place filter** (died-mode only): `FILTER NOT EXISTS { ?p wdt:P1082 [] }` inside the subquery drops places that carry a population. Wikidata records most deaths at city precision, and Denver at 25 km returned 148/150 rows on the city centroid; with the filter the results are hospitals, Stapleton, Denver Zoo. Measured live: 0.7 s Denver/25 km, 1.4 s Denver/90 km, 9.6 s Paris/25 km. The admin-entity path filter (`P31/P279* Q56061`) was rejected at 20–38 s. Buried-mode timing unchanged (Paris/90 mi cold: 3.3–4.3 s new shape vs 4.1 s old, within noise).
- **Explore** (`src/app/explore.tsx`): Buried here / Died here toggle above the radius chips (same chip recipe, selected state, visible label = accessible name); died-mode explainer paragraph as a single VoiceOver-reachable link to wikidata.org; mode-aware walk-up sub-line and empty state; mode folded into the scroll-to-top settle key.
- **Person page** (`src/app/person/[qid].tsx`): "Died at X" with "Buried at Y" sub in died-mode, "Resting at X" with "Died at Y" sub in buried-mode; visit control hidden for death places (visits stay grave-only — they feed ranks and processions); new 44pt "Improve this record on Wikidata" link to the item's Wikidata page; route-param parse defaults `placeKind`/`otherPlace` for pre-1.0.6 payloads.
- **Favorites** (`src/lib/favorites/types.ts`, `src/components/favorite-row.tsx`): `placeKind`/`otherPlace` optional on the persisted shape (AsyncStorage + Supabase `favorites.soul` jsonb) — no migration; `placeKindOfFav` is the single read-time default ("burial"). Saved rows prefix death places with "died ·".
- **Radius honored (both modes)** (`src/lib/wikidata.ts`, `src/app/explore.tsx`): the nearest-first subquery alone returned the same 150 downtown people at every radius in a dense city, so 90 mi looked like 5 mi. The inner query is now a UNION of two passes — nearest 150 by distance, plus an outer ring (25–100 % of the radius) of up to 90 people ordered by `MD5(STR(?person))` so the sample spreads across the whole ring instead of filling from its inner edge. Each `wikibase:around` pass scans every burial in the radius, so passes are the cost: Paris/145 km measured 22 s with four passes, 7.2 s with two; Denver 1.7 s; Denver 5 mi 0.5 s. Explore drops the 1.0.4 cap-aware framing: the header always says "within X" and the map always frames the radius. `capped` stays on `NearbyResult` as a density flag, unused by the UI. Sim: Denver 90 mi → 214 souls, Front Range from Fort Collins to Colorado Springs.
- **Wheel pickers** (`src/components/discovery-controls.native.tsx`, `discovery-controls.tsx`): the mode toggle and radius chips collapse into one row of two native iOS wheel pickers (`@expo/ui` SwiftUI `Picker`, wheel style, 96pt, Timer-app feel) — mode | radius side by side. Web keeps the chip rows via the `.tsx` fallback, mirroring the `souls-map` split. Mia's call during the test pass.
- **Died here explainer page** (`src/app/died-here.tsx`): the inline paragraph became a 44pt link row under the wheels (died-mode only: "Only recorded death spots are shown · How this works") that opens a Cemetourists-style page — what counts, why the list is shorter, how to add a place of death on Wikidata, with Open Wikidata / How to edit Wikidata buttons.
- **Search field keeps the picked place** (`src/components/place-search.tsx`): after picking a place it stays in the field (semibold) with the x to clear back to your location; the status row no longer appends "near <place>" (it wrapped to two lines), only "near you" / "Denver (sample)" when no place is picked. Tab re-select still clears the place and the field follows.
